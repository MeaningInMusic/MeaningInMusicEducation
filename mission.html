Mission

Why we make music

What we're here to do, and what we try to stick to while we do it.

Any kid who wants to learn music should get to, whatever their family can afford. We teach free lessons, lend donated instruments, and raise money for school music programs that need help.

Our core focuses

What we work on

Access

Give every child the chance to learn music and the opportunities it brings — no matter their financial situation.

Joy

Introduce the joy of music to more people — with lessons that are fun, personal, and one-on-one.

Fund programs in need

Raise donations, grants, and instrument drives to support school music programs that need it most.

Leadership

High school students plan and teach real lessons, growing into leaders, organizers, and engaged members of their community.

Our goals & values

What we try to stick to

  • Love of playing. We started this because we love music. We want kids to love it too, not just get through it.
  • Showing up. Lessons happen every week and instruments get delivered. We'd rather do small things reliably than big things once.
  • Keeping count. We track lessons taught, kids taught, and hours played, because that's how we know whether this is working.
  • Any kid, any music. Classical, pop, whatever a kid wants to play. Music belongs to every kid, whatever their background.
  • Relationships first. Kids keep playing when someone they look up to is in their corner. The relationship is the point.
