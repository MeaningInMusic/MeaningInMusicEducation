Ways to help

You don't have to play an instrument to be useful here. Here's what makes the biggest difference.

Apply for lessons

If you're a parent or guardian, sign your kid up. Lessons are free, one-on-one, and taught by a high schooler who plays their instrument.

Get started →

Teach as a high schooler

If you're in high school and play an instrument, teach a kid once a week. It counts toward service hours, and you'll learn more about music than you expect.

Reach out on our contact page

Volunteer

Help with events, paperwork, or picking up and dropping off instruments.

Donate

Money goes to instruments, repairs, and lesson books. So does an old violin from your closet.

Spread the word

Most families find us through a friend or a school post. Telling one family about us really does help.

Not sure where to start?

Send us a note about what you're good at, and we'll find something.
